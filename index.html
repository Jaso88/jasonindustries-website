About us

Meet Jason

Director & lead boilermaker

From the Bay to the Basin

Jason, the founder of Jason Industries Pty Ltd, grew up on the water at Jervis Bay on the New South Wales south coast. Work brought him north to Mackay, then to Airlie Beach, and back to Mackay, where the business is based today. That road from Jervis Bay to the Bowen Basin is where the motto comes from: From the Bay to the Basin.

He's a skilled boilermaker and proud business owner whose story reflects a lifelong commitment to precision, reliability and customer satisfaction, defined by craftsmanship, dedication and a strong work ethic.

With over a decade and a half of hands-on industry experience and a lifetime of learning from both sea and steel, Jason brings craftsmanship, leadership and integrity to every project. Whether it's urgent shutdown work or precision fabrication, Jason Industries delivers results that speak for themselves.

Quality over volume. Right the first time, every time.
